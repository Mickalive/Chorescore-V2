1. **Launch timing**: `sleep(1400)` → `sleep(3000)` in `launch()` — gives the app more time to start on the emulator.

3. **Wait timeouts**: `waitFor('Démonstration')` and `waitFor('Appartement démo')` raised to 60000ms; `waitFor('Ajouter une tâche')` raised to 30000ms.

These address the most likely root causes of the E2E failure: slow app launch, network state left disabled by the smoke script, and UI elements taking longer than the default 10s timeout to render.
